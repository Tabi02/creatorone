Questions

Do I still need Linktree, Calendly or a course platform?

No — that is the point. CreatorOne replaces those separate subscriptions with one page and one plan.

How do Instagram auto-DMs work?

Real auto-DMs need Meta's official API approval, which is a formal review process. The feature is a placeholder until that approval is in place — we do not use unofficial automation.

Can I accept payments?

Creating your page is free. For selling, there is a TEST checkout: a buyer can place a test order for your product or course, and you see it in your dashboard as pending until you verify it yourself. No real money is taken yet — real payments need a payment gateway deployment (YouPI in test review) plus your own merchant setup.

Where does my data live?

Your account, page, links, bookings, subscribers and analytics are stored in CreatorOne's database, so your page works from any device after you log in. Note: on the current free hosting tier, a redeploy can reset data — a managed database is the next step once the product has real users.

Is CreatorOne really free?

Yes — free for everyone. No card, no trial clock.
