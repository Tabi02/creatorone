Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026 · CreatorOne

What CreatorOne is

CreatorOne is a free all-in-one creator platform (link-in-bio, bookings, courses, analytics, email list and community on one page). When you create an account, your email, handle, page content, bookings, subscribers and analytics are stored in CreatorOne's database so your public page works from any device after you log in. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text. Payments are not connected yet, so no payment or card details are collected.

Information we collect

Account: email, handle, display name and bio. Page: links, products, courses and community posts you add. Visitors: name and email only when they book a call or subscribe on your page — those details are shown to the page owner in their dashboard. Analytics: page views and link clicks counted on your page. If you contact us by email, we will use your message only to reply to you.

Cookies and third-party advertising

This site may show advertisements served by Google AdSense in the future. When ads are live, Google and its partners may use cookies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time via Google Ads Settings, and you can read how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Until an ad code is placed on this site, no advertising cookies are set by us.

Analytics

No third-party analytics or tracking scripts are running at present. CreatorOne counts only your own page's views and link clicks, shown in your dashboard.

External links

Links on this site may point to third-party websites (for example, creator platforms). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.
